A DIFFERENT WAY TO LISTEN

Sound, with a little memory.

Choose Pool Light for sunlight refracted through rippling water, or Stanford Bunny for a softly responsive sculpture. Bring your own audio, shape its visual response, then keep a short film. The material label on the bunny switches between fur and copper.

  1. Bring a track. Press play for the demo, or choose an audio file. Up to 80 MB and 10 minutes.
  2. Find your form. Adjust the three controls. Drag the canvas to explore the view. Click or drag the waveform to revisit a moment.
  3. Keep what you make. Save your settings or export a 15- or 30-second film from the playhead. Clips stop at the end of the track.

Film recording happens in real time. Keep this tab visible until it finishes. Your settings file contains the controls and camera view; bring the same audio file to recreate the work.
